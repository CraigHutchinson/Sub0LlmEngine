Evidence & requirements

A slot minimum needs each slot's share of the context and the measured prompt + output to reach it: a speed measured at a short prompt is not evidence for a long one. Auto picks the largest standard window that keeps every measured concurrency.

Measured operating frontier

Outlined points are on the frontier: no other eligible point is at least as good on all three axes and better on one. A ring marks your selection. Select a dot or a row to inspect it.

How selection works and what the data proves

Only non-excluded successes with complete settings qualify. Repeated runs of the exact settings, depth, concurrency and output length use median throughput. Any recorded failure at that exact point excludes it from recommendations. Each axis is scaled linearly between the eligible cohort’s measured minimum and maximum; your priorities weight those three scores. Scores are relative rankings, not confidence or predicted speed. Samples may be short, correlated or warm-up affected. A synthetic batched benchmark does not validate output quality, server overhead, live memory headroom, or performance at untested depths. VRAM is a whole-process-run peak and can cover several concurrency rows.

Question the benchmark

Are there enough repeats? Does the requested output length match the measured one? Were the machine, model and build the same? Could warm-up, memory pressure or another process explain a failure? This coverage planner answers identity/repeat questions directly and allows a bounded verification run for memory hypotheses. It does not certify quality at extended context, estimate speeds in unmeasured regions, or erase conflicting evidence.
